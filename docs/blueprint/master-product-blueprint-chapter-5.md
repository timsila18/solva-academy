## 1. Executive Summary

Solva Academy should become one of the best places in Africa for qualified experts to build credible, sustainable, branded education businesses. Chapters 1 through 4 established the platform vision, identity model, learning engine, learner experience, AI Mentor, communities, and learner success architecture. Chapter 5 defines the complete instructor and academy-owner experience, from application and onboarding through teaching, learner management, live classes, academy operations, revenue, payouts, analytics, marketing, reputation, content ownership, and long-term tutor success.

Instructors must not be treated as people who only upload videos. Solva Academy should give them a teaching studio, business operating system, learner management workspace, Solco live teaching hub, marketplace storefront, analytics platform, AI-assisted content studio, and professional reputation engine.

This chapter defines:

- Instructor categories and tailored onboarding.
- Instructor application, review, verification, and launch workflows.
- Instructor dashboards, public profiles, and workspaces.
- AI Teaching Studio.
- Course operations and teaching teams.
- Learner management, assignment review, live Solco teaching, office hours, and coaching.
- Academy owner dashboards, operations, and team management.
- Revenue models, revenue sharing, earnings ledgers, payouts, refunds, disputes, and financial transparency.
- Instructor and academy analytics, course health, marketing tools, coupons, referrals, and reputation.
- Instructor success programs, launch support, platform support, content ownership, copyright protection, and content portability.
- Academy storefronts, educational marketplace products, corporate sales leads, notifications, admin controls, fraud prevention, and conceptual data entities.

The product direction is clear: Solva Academy should help experts teach well, support learners well, earn transparently, grow ethically, and build long-term education brands.

## 4. Application and Onboarding

Instructor onboarding should be professional, configurable, and supportive. It should filter for quality without making legitimate experts feel punished by bureaucracy.

### Suggested Workflow

1. User selects "Become an Instructor."
2. User reviews instructor benefits and expectations.
3. User selects teaching category.
4. User completes instructor profile.
5. User adds experience and qualifications.
6. User uploads supporting documents where required.
7. User submits sample lesson, introduction video, or teaching outline.
8. User selects preferred payout method.
9. User accepts instructor terms.
10. Application enters review.
11. Reviewer approves, requests changes, conditionally approves, or rejects.
12. Approved instructor completes onboarding checklist.
13. Instructor creates first course or joins an academy.
14. Instructor receives launch guidance.
15. Instructor publishes after relevant course review.

### Configurable Requirements

Super Admin should configure required steps and fields by:

- School.
- Category.
- Risk level.
- Instructor type.
- Country.
- Age group served.

### Onboarding Checklist

Approved instructors should receive a checklist covering:

- Profile completion.
- Verification status.
- Payout setup.
- Instructor terms.
- Course creation basics.
- Quality standards.
- Child safety where relevant.
- Solco setup where relevant.
- AI Studio orientation.
- First course launch steps.

## 46. Risks and Mitigations

### Risk: Instructor Experience Becomes Upload-Only

If the platform only supports uploads and payouts, it will not create sustainable instructor businesses.

Mitigation:

- Build teaching workflows, learner management, analytics, marketing, coaching, marketplace, and academy operations into the instructor experience.

### Risk: Financial Confusion

Unclear revenue splits and payout timing can damage trust.

Mitigation:

- Provide transparent ledgers, payout timelines, fee breakdowns, refund impact, and downloadable statements.

### Risk: Low-Quality or Misleading Courses

Poor content harms learners and platform reputation.

Mitigation:

- Use verification, quality review, health scores, learner feedback, and category-specific reviewer requirements.

### Risk: Fraudulent Instructor Claims

Fake qualifications can harm learners.

Mitigation:

- Require configurable verification workflows, document review, badges with precise meaning, and revocation processes.

### Risk: Payout Abuse

Fraudsters may exploit payouts, referrals, or fake activity.

Mitigation:

- Use payout holds, risk flags, identity verification, affiliate fraud checks, and audit logs.

### Risk: Creator Rights Disputes

Unclear ownership or copyright rules can create legal and trust issues.

Mitigation:

- Define content ownership, platform license, takedown workflow, counter-notice workflow, and repeat infringement rules.

### Risk: Child Safety Incidents

Instructor contact with minors requires strict safeguards.

Mitigation:

- Use child-safe teaching approval, messaging restrictions, reporting, moderation, and safeguarding escalation.
